AI visibility

Your next client is asking an AI, not Google. Make sure it names you.

People have stopped scanning ten blue links. They ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity and get one answer with two or three recommendations. That answer names either your company or your competitor.

These systems read a website differently from Google: they don't run JavaScript, don't wait for the page to finish rendering, and only use what they can verify. An ordinary site has nothing to give them. So we build a machine-readable layer into every site we ship, and this site is the proof.

  • llms.txt: a site map written for language models: services, prices and contacts on one page
  • Every page also served as clean markdown via the Accept: text/markdown header, with no HTML noise
  • Schema.org JSON-LD: company, registration number, services, prices and region as verifiable data, not as a sentence in a paragraph
  • MCP server card in .well-known plus agent skills: the site can serve your customer's AI agent
  • Static pages, no JavaScript: a crawler reads the whole content on the first pass, PageSpeed 95–100
  • Four languages with hreflang: Czech, Russian, Ukrainian, English. Your customer gets the answer in their own
